## Decision

No candidate satisfies both economic scopes, paired V1 improvement,
drawdown/profit-factor gates, required-regime economics, and calibration gates.
No `model.joblib` is retained in any candidate directory. The production swing
and intraday APIs remain not-ready and must not fall back to these artifacts.

The next hypothesis must change the setup population or causal information
available at decision time. Additional estimator complexity on the same rows
is not justified by this evidence.
